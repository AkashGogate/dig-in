Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: finished restaurant menu poster, portrait, high resolution.
Create an exceptionally well-designed, readable, construction-themed food menu for a hackathon demo restaurant called DIG IN. Output the flat, front-facing artwork itself, with no photographic mockup, no table, no frame, no perspective.

Art direction: bold industrial construction signage meets a beautifully typeset independent burger restaurant menu. Safety yellow, near-black ink, and warm ivory. Large oversized tightly set condensed block type for DIG IN at the top, small uppercase monospaced utility labels, clear substantial sans serif dish names and comfortably readable descriptions. Mostly warm ivory background with black text; a broad yellow masthead, yellow section highlights and narrow black/yellow diagonal hazard-stripe bands at top and bottom. Subtle printed paper grain, crisp graphic edges, generous margins. A small tasteful black line illustration of an excavator with a fork-like bucket near the title; a tiny hardhat or shovel motif in the footer. Decorative marks remain minimal and support the typography. No food photography, no 3D rendering, no gradients, no fake signage distressing that harms reading. Professional, witty, inviting, strong typography, not childish or cluttered.

Layout: portrait page, title masthead occupies roughly top fifth. Beneath it, three clearly separated full-width horizontal menu sections in a single-column layout with ample whitespace. Each section has a small numbered yellow-and-black marker and section heading. Dish names bold, descriptions one smaller line or two directly below. Exactly 3 appetizers, exactly 3 mains, exactly 2 desserts, each appearing once. All eight items must be fully visible. No prices. Footer reads BUILT FOR BIG APPETITES. Do not invent any additional dishes, symbols for dietary claims, dates, locations, offers, or prices.
